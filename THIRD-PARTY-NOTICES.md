## Original work in this repository

`LidWatch.kext` and `SSDT-LIDW.aml`, `SSDT-YOGAPM.aml`, the two DSDT patches that go with it, everything in `tools/`, the build scripts, and the documentation. The remaining SSDTs are the standard hackintosh ones described in [Dortania's ACPI guide](https://dortania.github.io/Getting-Started-With-ACPI/).
